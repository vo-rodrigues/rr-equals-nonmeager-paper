# Manuscript

*Determining the Rearrangement Number*, by Vinicius de Oliveira Rodrigues.

The manuscript proves **rr = non(M)** in ZFC. Its source entry point is
[main.tex](main.tex); the preamble and bibliography are in [includes/](includes/),
and the five sections are in [sections/](sections/).

## Formalization and citation

The accompanying [Lean project](../lean/README.md) formalizes **non(M) ≤ rr**.
See its [correspondence document](../lean/docs/MANUSCRIPT.md) for precise scope.
