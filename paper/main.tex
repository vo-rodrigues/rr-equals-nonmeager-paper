\begin{abstract}
The rearrangement number $\rr$ is the least cardinality of a collection of
permutations of $\omega$ such that every conditionally convergent real
series is disrupted by some permutation in the collection.  Blass, Brendle,
Brian, Hamkins, Hardy, and Larson proved that $\max\{\covN,\bb\}\leq\rr\leq\nonM$
and asked whether $\rr=\nonM$.
We prove in ZFC that $\rr=\nonM$.
\end{abstract}
